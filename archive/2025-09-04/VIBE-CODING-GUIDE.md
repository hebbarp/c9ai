# 🎭 Vibe Coding: How to Use C9AI's Universal Vibe Task Manager

## What is Vibe Coding?

**Vibe Coding** is a revolutionary approach to development that matches your current energy, mood, and context with optimal workflow templates. Instead of fighting against your natural rhythms, you work **WITH** your vibe to maximize productivity and enjoyment.

## 🚀 Quick Start Guide

### 1. Access Vibe Sessions
- Open C9AI in your browser
- Click **🎭 Vibe Sessions** in the left sidebar
- You'll see the Universal Vibe Task Manager interface

### 2. Detect Your Current Vibe
Fill out the vibe detection form:
- **Energy Level**: High Energy 🚀, Focused 🎯, Medium ⚡, Low Energy 🌙
- **Current Mood**: Creative 🎨, Analytical 🔬, Productive ⚡, Exploratory 🗺️, Collaborative 👥, Steady 📊
- **Available Time**: 30 minutes to 3+ hours
- **Work Context**: Home Office 🏠, Quiet Space 🤫, Team Environment 👥, Dual Monitors 💻, Mobile 📱

Click **🔍 Detect My Vibe** to get AI-powered workflow recommendations.

### 3. Choose Your Workflow
Based on your detected vibe, you'll see matching templates:
- **Morning Creative** 🌅 - Fresh energy content creation
- **Deep Analysis** 🔬 - Data diving and systematic thinking  
- **Rapid Prototype** ⚡ - Fast building and experimentation

### 4. Start Interactive Session
Click **"Start This Workflow"** on any template to begin an interactive session.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Vibe Detection Not Working**
- Check that C9AI server is running on port 8787
- Ensure all form fields are filled out
- Try refreshing the page and re-detecting

**Template Not Loading**
- Click "Refresh Templates" button
- Check browser console for errors
- Verify workflow API endpoints are responding

**Step Execution Failing**
- Check that you have credits for AI-powered steps
- Verify file uploads are in supported formats
- Ensure custom instructions are clear and actionable

**Results Not Appearing**
- Wait for streaming to complete (may take 30-60 seconds)
- Check that step completed successfully (green checkmark)
- Try refreshing the session if results seem stuck

### Getting Help
- Use the **❓ Help** section in C9AI for documentation
- Check the browser console for technical errors
- Save your session before troubleshooting to preserve progress
