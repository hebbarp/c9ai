## Data Verification Framework

### Multi-Source Cross-Reference Process

```
Company Identification
↓
Source 1: Tracxn (funding/employees)
↓  
Source 2: LinkedIn (headcount verification)
↓
Source 3: Glassdoor (employee reviews)
↓
Source 4: Company website (careers page)
↓
Final: Annual reports (if available)
```

**Requirement**: Minimum 2 sources for database inclusion

## Company Segmentation Strategy

### Tier Classification System

**Tier 1**: 10,000+ employees
- Global MNCs (Microsoft, Google, Amazon)
- Highest payroll service spend potential

**Tier 2**: 1,000-10,000 employees  
- Large established companies (TCS, Infosys, Wipro)
- Consistent payroll outsourcing needs

**Tier 3**: 500-1,000 employees
- Mid-size growth companies
- Emerging payroll service buyers
