# Tiny Dickinson Notes

This is the first learning sample for Small Language Foundry.

Training target:

- style adaptation
- short-form generation
- refusal to invent quotes
- clear separation between "inspired by a corpus" and impersonation

Suggested first trainer:

- Unsloth or LLaMA-Factory
- small instruct base model
- QLoRA adapter first
- export to GGUF/Ollama after the loop is understood

Do not optimize for benchmark scores. Optimize for whether the before/after behavior is visibly different and still honest.
